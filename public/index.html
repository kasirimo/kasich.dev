THE PROBLEM

Is Your Business Losing Customers Online?

Your customers are already online. If your business isn't giving them a fast, professional, and convenient digital experience, they may be choosing someone else.

01 LOW ONLINE VISIBILITY
People can't find you.

Your business exists, but potential customers struggle to discover you online when they're searching for the products or services you offer.

02 OUTDATED WEBSITE
Your website is hurting your brand.

An outdated website can make your business look less professional, reduce trust, and send potential customers somewhere else.

03 NO ONLINE SALES
Customers can't buy from you online.

If customers have to call, visit, or message you just to place an order, you're making the buying process harder than it needs to be.

04 POOR MOBILE EXPERIENCE
Your website doesn't work well on phones.

Most people browse from their phones. If your website is difficult to navigate or use on mobile, you're potentially losing customers.

05 MANUAL BOOKING PROCESS
You're still managing bookings manually.

Constant calls, messages, spreadsheets, and back-and-forth conversations can waste time and create unnecessary mistakes.

06 LACK OF CUSTOMER ENGAGEMENT
Customers visit once and disappear.

Without effective ways to communicate with customers, follow up, and keep them engaged, valuable opportunities can be lost.

07 SLOW WEBSITE
Your website is making your customers wait.

Slow-loading pages create frustration and can cause visitors to leave before they ever see what your business offers.

08 NO ONLINE PRESENCE
Your business isn't online yet.

If customers can't find your business online, you may be invisible to people who are actively looking for what you offer.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of businesses do you work with?

I work with businesses, professionals, startups, and organizations that need a stronger online presence or a custom digital solution.

Do you only build websites?

No. I build websites as well as custom web applications, e-commerce platforms, booking systems, dashboards, and business management solutions.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, functionality, design requirements, and complexity of the project. Packages are available for common needs, while custom projects are quoted individually.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the project. A simple business website may take significantly less time than a custom web application.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I can redesign and modernize an existing website while improving its responsiveness, usability, performance, and overall appearance.

Can you maintain my website after launch?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance, updates, improvements, and additional features can be arranged after launch.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Projects can be handled remotely with communication, feedback, and project updates managed online.
